# Ancient Civilizations

## Mesopotamia: The Cradle of Civilization

### Geography and Context
- Located between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers (modern-day Iraq)
- Fertile Crescent region
- Known as the "land between rivers"
- Rich soil ideal for agriculture

### Key Civilizations of Mesopotamia

#### Sumerians (c. 4500–1900 BCE)
- First major civilization in Mesopotamia
- Invented cuneiform writing (one of the earliest writing systems)
- Developed city-states (Ur, Uruk, Lagash)
- Built ziggurats — massive temple complexes
- Created the wheel and the plow

#### Akkadian Empire (c. 2334–2154 BCE)
- Led by Sargon the Great
- First empire in history
- United Sumerian city-states under one rule
- Created the first standing army

#### Babylonian Empire (c. 1894–539 BCE)
- Famous ruler: Hammurabi (c. 1792–1750 BCE)
- Hammurabi's Code — one of the earliest legal codes
- Code contained 282 laws based on "an eye for an eye"
- Babylon became a major cultural and trade center

#### Assyrian Empire (c. 1365–609 BCE)
- Known for their military might and iron weapons
- Built the Library of Ashurbanipal at Nineveh
- Developed an extensive road system
- Used siege warfare and advanced military tactics

### Contributions of Mesopotamia
- Writing (cuneiform)
- The wheel
- Mathematics (base-60 system, 360-degree circle)
- Astronomy and astrology
- Epic of Gilgamesh (oldest known literary work)

## Ancient Egypt

### Geography and Context
- Centered along the Nile River
- Nile provided fertile soil through annual floods
- Natural barriers (deserts, seas) provided protection
- Divided into Upper Egypt (south) and Lower Egypt (north)

### Historical Periods

#### Old Kingdom (c. 2686–2181 BCE)
- Age of the pyramid builders
- Great Pyramid of Giza built for Pharaoh Khufu
- Strong central government
- Period of peace and prosperity

#### Middle Kingdom (c. 2055–1650 BCE)
- Period of reunification and cultural flourishing
- Expansion of trade routes
- Development of irrigation systems
- Literature and art thrived

#### New Kingdom (c. 1550–1069 BCE)
- Age of empire and military expansion
- Famous pharaohs: Hatshepsut, Akhenaten, Tutankhamun, Ramesses II
- Construction of the Valley of the Kings
- Egypt reached its greatest territorial extent

### Egyptian Society
- **Pharaoh**: Absolute ruler, considered a living god
- **Vizier**: Chief administrator
- **Priests**: Managed temples and religious rituals
- **Scribes**: Recorded information and managed bureaucracy
- **Artisans and Merchants**: Produced goods and facilitated trade
- **Farmers and Laborers**: Majority of the population

### Egyptian Contributions
- Hieroglyphic writing
- Papyrus for writing material
- Advanced mathematics and geometry
- Medical knowledge and surgical techniques
- Mummification practices
- Solar calendar with 365 days

## Indus Valley Civilization

### Geography and Context
- Located in present-day Pakistan and northwest India
- Centered around the Indus River
- Major cities: Harappa and Mohenjo-Daro

### Key Characteristics
- Highly organized city planning with grid layouts
- Advanced drainage and sanitation systems
- Standardized weights and measures
- Extensive trade networks with Mesopotamia
- Written language (still undeciphered)

### Indus Valley Society
- Evidence of social stratification
- Skilled artisans (pottery, beads, seals)
- Religious artifacts suggest nature worship
- No clear evidence of a powerful monarchy

### Decline (c. 1900–1300 BCE)
- Possible causes: climate change, river shifting, invasion
- Gradual decline rather than sudden collapse

## Ancient China

### Geography and Context
- Centered along the Yellow River (Huang He) and Yangtze River
- Natural barriers: mountains, deserts, Pacific Ocean
- Relatively isolated development

### Early Dynasties

#### Shang Dynasty (c. 1600–1046 BCE)
- First historically verified Chinese dynasty
- Developed bronze casting technology
- Used oracle bones for divination
- Feudal social structure with king at the top

#### Zhou Dynasty (c. 1046–256 BCE)
- Introduced the Mandate of Heaven concept
- Developed feudalism
- Period of ironworking advancement
- Late Zhou period: Warring States era
- Philosophical flourishing: Confucianism, Daoism, Legalism

### Chinese Contributions
- Silk production
- Paper making
- Magnetic compass
- Gunpowder
- Advanced bronze and iron casting
